About

Band Bio

Moon Scorched is an international hardcore band from Hamm, Germany, united by different cultural backgrounds and one shared vision: honest, heavy, and unapologetically intense hardcore.

Built on crushing riffs, relentless grooves, and explosive breakdowns, their sound is direct, aggressive, and impossible to ignore. Influenced by bands like Desolated, Guilt Trip, and Hard Reset, Moon Scorched channel anger, determination, and raw emotion into songs that never let up.

The stage is where the band truly comes alive. Every show is an explosion. Loud, relentless, and honest. Every riff carries weight, every breakdown demands a reaction, and every moment is driven by the connection between the band and the crowd.

No theatrics. No pretending. Just pure intensity from the first note to the last.

Raw energy in motion.
