About

Band Bio

Four people. Different backgrounds. One sound. Moon Scorched is an international hardcore band from Hamm, Germany.

Built on crushing riffs, relentless grooves, two-steps and explosive breakdowns, Moon Scorched turn anger, determination and raw emotion into direct, uncompromising hardcore.

Influenced by bands like Desolated, Guilt Trip and Hard Reset, the band combines heavy grooves with the kind of energy that only really makes sense when a room starts moving.

The stage is where Moon Scorched comes alive. Loud, relentless and honest. No theatrics. No pretending. Just intensity from the first note to the last.
