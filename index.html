Everyday

“Everyday” is a song about being trapped in the endless repetition of modern life. Wake up, work, spend money on things you don’t really need, sleep, and do it all over again.

It’s about the exhaustion of constantly working without knowing what you’re actually working for. About watching days turn into weeks, months, and years while feeling like you’re running in circles — always busy, always chasing something, but never really getting anywhere.

If this is what life is supposed to be, what the fuck is the point?

About

Band Bio

Four people. Different backgrounds. One sound. Moon Scorched is an international hardcore band from Hamm, Germany.

Moon Scorched turn frustration, pressure and raw emotion into direct, uncompromising hardcore — built on crushing riffs, relentless grooves, two-steps and explosive breakdowns.

With “Everyday”, the band takes aim at the cycle of modern life: working, consuming, sleeping and repeating while constantly being told to keep moving.

Influenced by bands like Desolated, Guilt Trip and Hard Reset, Moon Scorched combine heavy grooves with the kind of energy that only really makes sense when a room starts moving.

Loud, relentless and honest. No theatrics. No pretending. Just intensity from the first note to the last.
